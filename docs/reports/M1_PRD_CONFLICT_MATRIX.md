## Interpretation Rules

The production PRD is the owner-approved production execution source of truth. Legacy documents remain historical until deliberately reconciled. This matrix does not silently rewrite or discard their business rules. A `REQUIRES DECISION` item must receive owner approval through `docs/DECISIONS.md` before implementation. Historical test counts and milestone labels are not current evidence.

Allowed classifications:

- `COMPATIBLE`: materially aligned with the production PRD.
- `STALE HISTORICAL CLAIM`: may describe an earlier run or plan but lacks current applicability/evidence.
- `SUPERSEDED`: replaced by an explicit production PRD requirement.
- `REQUIRES DECISION`: presents a genuine alternative that the PRD or owner has not resolved.
- `MISSING IMPLEMENTATION`: required behavior or artifact is absent/incomplete.
- `MISSING EVIDENCE`: implementation may exist, but fresh proof required by the PRD is absent.

## Preserved Business Rules

These current owner rules are preserved and must not be changed during reconciliation:

- The booking party acts as customer.
- A vehicle owner/driver acts as vendor.
- A transport company may act as customer or vendor per transaction; the selected role is explicit and audited.
- Refunds require manual approval.
- Agents cannot approve their own actions.
- Deterministic pricing and state transitions remain outside LLM authority.
