## Design and security controls

- **Disabled by default:** both the adapter and the worker settings default to `enabled=False` and `sampling_rate=0.0`.
- **No Langfuse client creation:** the M6-A adapter does not instantiate a Langfuse client or SDK.
- **No network or socket use:** sinks are in-memory or explicitly failing; no HTTP/TCP/UDP calls.
- **No live sink:** only `NullSink`, `InMemorySink`, and `FailingSink` are provided.
- **Strict typed allowlist:** metadata cannot carry arbitrary keys; unknown fields are classified as prohibited.
- **No prompts/responses/exception content:** `raw_prompt`, `raw_response`, `system_prompt`, `prompt_body`, `response_body`, `input_text`, `output_text`, `exception_message`, `stack_trace`, `error_message` are all prohibited or restricted.
- **No PII, business records, payment/Odoo/Paperclip payloads:** names, addresses, emails, phones, amounts, Razorpay/Odoo/Paperclip identifiers, ledger entries, and secrets are prohibited.
- **Server-generated IDs:** `trace_id`, `span_id`, and `correlation_id` are generated by the adapter; clients cannot set `trace_id`.
- **HMAC-SHA256 pseudonymization:** raw tenant/actor/workflow identifiers are hashed with an injected 64-hex-character pepper; raw values never reach sinks.
- **Fail-open delivery:** sink exceptions are swallowed; business operations continue unaffected.
- **Fail-closed redaction:** if a field is prohibited or unclassifiable, the telemetry event is dropped while the business operation continues.
- **No business impact:** telemetry has no write methods, no retry logic, and cannot alter business results.
- **No database writes:** the M6-A module performs no database or external-state writes.
- **No runtime integration outside worker boundary:** handlers and entrypoints remain unchanged.
