# Zippy Logistics — Milestone M2: Deterministic Pricing, Matching & Payment Rules

## Objective
Implement the fully deterministic business core that M1's schema enables: the pricing engine, provider matching RPC, and payment-rule enforcement — all as DB functions so every app and agent gets identical numbers.

## Entry Criteria
- M1 complete (schema + state machine live-verified).

## Design Decisions (deterministic, no ambiguity)

### Pricing (SOP-sourced rate bands)
| Vehicle class | Tons | Rate/km band (₹) |
|---|---|---|
| Mini Truck | 0.5 – 2 | 10 – 25 |
| LCV | 2 – 7 | 15 – 40 |
| MCV | 9 – 12 | 20 – 30 |
| HCV | 20 – 40 | 35 – 85 |

- **Weight interpolation** (deterministic): `rate = min_rate + (w - min_t)/(max_t - min_t) × (max_rate - min_rate)`; NULL weight → band midpoint.
  - Verified fixture: 10 t @ 120 km → MCV, rate **23.33**, freight **2800**, toll 415, loading 84, GST 164.95, total **3463.95**.
- Weight > class max → raise `CARGO_EXCEEDS_CLASS_CAPACITY`; > 40 t total → `CARGO_EXCEEDS_MAX_CAPACITY`. Distance ≤ 0 → `INVALID_DISTANCE`.
- **Toll bands (fixed floors, PRD-derived)**: ≤50 km ₹85 · 51–250 ₹415 · 251–800 ₹1575 · >800 ₹2600.
- **Loading/unloading** = 3% of freight. **GST** = 5% of (freight + toll + loading).
- `total = freight + toll + loading + tax`. `orders.total_amount` update re-fires commission trigger automatically.

### Matching (D-05 identity pin)
`match_nearby_drivers` returns **users.user_id** as the assignable identity (matches `orders.provider_id`),
plus driver_id/vehicle_id for display. Deterministic score:
`score = rating × 10 − distance_km × 0.05`, filtered to online+active vehicles with
`capacity_tons ≥ cargo_weight` (if cargo specified) and nearest-first tie-break.

### Payment rules
- `validate_payment_plan(mode,total,advance)`: full ⇒ advance=total; partial ⇒ advance ≥ 50% total; to_pay ⇒ advance=0 pre-delivery.
- DB-enforced: BEFORE INSERT guard on `orders` blocks customers with `payment_hold=true`
  unless an unexpired `admin_action('allow_user_with_pending_payment')` targets them.
